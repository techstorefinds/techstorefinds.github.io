Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a UPS?

A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) provides temporary battery backup during power outages and helps protect your devices from sudden power loss and voltage fluctuations.

2. How do I choose the right UPS?

Choose a UPS based on the power capacity (VA), battery backup time, number of output sockets, backup type, and the devices you plan to connect.

3. Can a UPS power a Wi-Fi router?

Yes. Most UPS models can provide backup power to Wi-Fi routers, allowing your internet connection to continue during short power outages.

4. How long does a UPS battery last?

A UPS battery typically lasts between 3 and 5 years, depending on usage, operating conditions, and maintenance.
