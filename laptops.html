Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which laptop is best for everyday use?

The Dell 15, HP 15, and Acer Aspire 3 are suitable for everyday tasks such as browsing, online classes, office work, and watching videos. Choose based on your preferred screen size, processor, and budget.

2. Is 8GB RAM enough for everyday use?

Yes, 8GB RAM is generally enough for everyday activities like web browsing, document editing, online meetings, and studying. Heavy multitasking and demanding applications may need more RAM.

3. Is a 512GB SSD enough for a laptop?

A 512GB SSD provides good storage for documents, applications, photos, and videos. It also helps the laptop start up and load applications quickly.

4. Which is better, a 14-inch or 15.6-inch laptop?

A 14-inch laptop is usually easier to carry, making it suitable for students and people who travel. A 15.6-inch laptop offers a larger screen, which can be more comfortable for working, studying, and watching videos.
