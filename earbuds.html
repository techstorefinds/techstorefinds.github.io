Frequently Asked Questions

1. What should I consider before buying earbuds?

Consider sound quality, battery life, comfort, connectivity, water resistance, and features like active noise cancellation.

2. Are wireless earbuds good for calls?

Yes. Many modern earbuds include built-in microphones with noise reduction for clear voice calls.

3. What is Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)?

ANC helps reduce surrounding noise, making it easier to enjoy music or calls in noisy environments.

4. Are earbuds suitable for workouts?

Yes. Earbuds with an IPX4 or higher water-resistance rating are suitable for workouts and light rain.
