Frequently Asked Questions

1. What capacity power bank should I buy?

Choose the capacity based on your charging needs. 10,000 mAh is suitable for everyday use, while 20,000 mAh is better for longer trips.

2. Is higher charging speed better?

Yes, higher wattage can charge compatible devices faster, but your device must support the required charging standard.

3. How many devices can a power bank charge?

It depends on the number of output ports and the power bank's supported output.

4. What should I check before buying a power bank?

Check the capacity, charging speed, ports, size, battery type, and safety features.
